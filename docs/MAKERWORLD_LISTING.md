# MakerWorld listing draft — MK CNC (WIP)

## Title
MK CNC — scavenger gantry mill from leftover 3D-printer parts (WIP)

## Summary
Small DIY gantry CNC from retired printer leftovers (2020, MGN12, T8, NEMA). Printable interfaces, BOM, assembly docs. Envelope ~299×271×100 mm. Work in progress — expect changes.

## Description
⚠️ Work in progress / early release

This project is still under active development. Parts, fits, docs, and print projects may change. Something may not fit perfectly on your leftover hardware, and dimensions or STLs can be updated without notice. Build at your own risk and expect to tweak.

What it is
A documented scavenger gantry mill: turn leftover 3D-printer parts into a useful small CNC for wood, soft plastics, and light aluminium (conservative feeds). Not a commercial kit and not a cast-iron machine.

What you get
- Printable parts (STL, print orientation)
- Bambu Studio multi-plate projects for P1S and A1 mini
- BOM, printing guide, assembly, overview renders
- Licence: CERN-OHL-P-2.0

Quick specs
- Envelope (nominal): X 299 × Y 271 × Z 100 mm
- Stack: 2020 extrusion + MGN12 + T8 + NEMA steppers
- Default tool: ø52 mm ~500 W spindle (ER11); optional Makita / laser / pen / dust shoe
